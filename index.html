FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear commercial terms before development begins.

These answers set the baseline for how InHaus Technology scopes, funds, delivers and protects professional engineering work.

Professional engagement

Funded engineering first. Optional participation by agreement.

Development and project delivery are the company's core business and source of income. Work is reserved through funded scopes, retainers and agreed payments. InHaus Technology may also propose an optional success participation on a project, but there is no obligation to accept it: the structure and figures are negotiable and apply only when agreed in writing.

What payment methods does InHaus Technology accept?

InHaus Technology accepts USDC or an agreed blockchain network's main native token, such as ETH, SOL or BNB. The asset, network, valuation method, payment schedule and wallet details are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Is a retainer required before work starts?

Yes. Development capacity is reserved only after an agreed upfront retainer has cleared. The retainer is a percentage of the project total, is recorded in the Scope of Works and is credited toward the agreed project cost.

How is the project scope agreed and managed?

InHaus Technology and the client approve a written Scope of Works covering deliverables, features, integrations, responsibilities, dependencies, milestones, timelines, payment stages, assumptions and exclusions.

Any new feature, integration, redesign, expanded requirement or additional delivery stage is treated as a scope variation and quoted separately before that work begins. Corrections required to deliver the originally agreed scope remain part of the original engagement.

How quickly can InHaus Technology deliver?

InHaus Technology moves quickly once a project is scoped, funded and ready. To maintain momentum, clients must provide repositories, hosting and domain access, wallets, contracts, API credentials, brand assets, approved content, technical documentation, decisions and approvals when required.

Missing dependencies, delayed decisions or changing requirements can pause work and extend the agreed timeframe.

Why can a project be completed quickly when the quoted cost is substantial?

A short delivery window does not mean the client is receiving less value. The agreed fee reflects the required outcome, scope, responsibility, technical risk and specialist capability—not an obligation to make the work take longer.

Time and effort still create value, including the years invested before an engagement begins. InHaus Technology draws on 23 years of technical experience across IT, business systems, database administration, disaster recovery, software engineering and blockchain systems. A project delivered in a week can sit on top of decades of learning, testing, fault recovery and production decision-making.

Fast delivery is the result of accumulated knowledge, established methods and focused effort. When a strong outcome is achieved sooner than expected, that efficiency should be recognised as part of the value—not treated as a reason to reduce it.

What can InHaus Technology build?

InHaus Technology can design, develop, audit, investigate and improve a broad range of applications and technical systems, including full-stack platforms, blockchain applications, smart contracts, payment infrastructure, trading systems, wallets, APIs, data services, deployment systems, simulations, games and operational tools.

When a project requires unfamiliar technology, the company can research, test and develop the required capability where it is technically practical, safe and commercially viable. Unsupported promises are not made.

Does InHaus Technology use AI?

Absolutely. AI assists with development work including research, code generation, debugging, testing, documentation, technical analysis, prototyping and automation.

AI is like a baseball bat. Give the same bat to a beginner and to Babe Ruth: the tool is identical, but the result is not. The bat does not create expertise; it amplifies the skill of the person holding it.

AI complements engineering skill; it does not become the skill itself. Experienced developers know what questions to ask, how to challenge an answer, when the output is wrong and what must be validated before it reaches production.

InHaus Technology uses AI as a powerful engineering tool, never as a replacement for architecture, technical judgement, security review, product understanding or accountability. The better the understanding of the work, the more effectively the tool can be used.

Does InHaus Technology request a participation in projects it helps build?

InHaus Technology may propose an optional success participation on any project it helps build. This may take the form of an agreed revenue share, token allocation, equity interest or another clearly defined commercial interest.

There is no obligation to accept. The client may agree, decline or negotiate the structure, percentage, duration and reporting terms. No participation applies unless both parties approve it in writing.

Any participation is separate from the retainer, Scope of Works and development fees. It recognises shared project success; it does not replace payment for funded engineering or create an obligation for unpaid future work.

Can InHaus Technology join our team or collaborate for future value?

InHaus Technology can work alongside a project team as an external development and project-management provider under a funded commercial agreement.

Project owners must provide a clear scope, available budget, required outcome and decision-making structure. Suitable enquiries are reviewed on their merits, and repeated follow-up does not create a commitment or reserve development time.

Does InHaus Technology join project teams or work for future promises?

InHaus Technology does not join project teams or commit development resources in exchange for promises of future payment, exposure, token value, equity, revenue, launch proceeds or success-based compensation alone.

Development and project delivery are the company's core business and source of income. Work therefore proceeds under a funded commercial agreement with an agreed Scope of Works, retainer and payment stages.

InHaus Technology can work closely alongside a client's internal team as an external development and project-delivery partner. A separately negotiated optional revenue share, token allocation, equity interest or other success participation may also be agreed, but it is additional to—and never a replacement for—funded engineering fees. No such arrangement applies unless confirmed in writing.

How does InHaus Technology approach financial ethics?

Financial behaviour must be transparent, technically accurate and honestly represented to users. InHaus Technology will not knowingly build systems based on misleading balances, concealed deductions, manipulated outcomes, false financial claims or unauthorised movement of funds.

Where a proposal creates ethical, security or operational concerns, the company will explain the issue, why it matters and what must change to make the system responsible and defensible.

Why might InHaus Technology decline a project?

A project may be declined because of missing payment arrangements, unresolved retainers, speculative compensation, ethical concerns, unsafe financial behaviour, an undefined or continually changing scope, missing access, unrealistic expectations or an inability to make timely decisions.

Projects that are funded, clearly scoped, ethically sound and operationally ready are far more likely to be accepted.

Why did InHaus Technology stop working with a previous project or community?

Development relationships can end when commercial or operational commitments are not met, including unpaid invoices, missing retainers, uncontrolled scope changes or unavailable decision-makers.

InHaus Technology does not publish private client financial details. A public claim that a developer simply disappeared may not include the complete contractual, payment or delivery history.

Can InHaus Technology take over or recover an existing project?

Potentially. Existing systems can be assessed, repaired, completed or restructured. Recovery work normally begins with a paid review of source-code access, deployment state, wallet and contract control, documentation, technical debt, security risks, required outcomes and available budget.
